What an annuity can do

Ways an annuity can help your retirement

Every product is different. Your agent will explain which of these fit you.

Monthly income for lifeYour savings can become a paycheck that lasts as long as you do.
Protection from market dropsFixed and indexed annuities don't lose value when the market falls.
A locked-in rateSome annuities pay a set rate for a set number of years.
Tax-deferred growthYou don't pay tax on growth until you take money out.
Income for your spouseJoint options can keep paying while either of you is alive.
Money for your familyMany products let you leave what's left to the people you choose.

Annuities are insurance products, not bank deposits, and are not FDIC insured. Guarantees depend on the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company. Annuities may have surrender charges and fees if you withdraw early. Withdrawals may be taxable, and a 10% federal penalty may apply before age 59½. We do not give tax or legal advice.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. There is no fee for the consultation, and you don't have to buy anything.

How do you make money if it's free for me?

The licensed agencies in our network pay us to connect them with people who want help. If you buy a product, the agent is paid a commission by the insurance company. You don't pay us anything.

Who will contact me, and how many calls will I get?

We match you with one licensed agent in your state. That agent will call or text you to book your free consultation. You can stop calls and texts at any time by asking the agent or replying STOP.

What is an annuity?

An annuity is a contract with an insurance company. You give it a lump sum or a series of payments. In return it can grow your money, protect it, or pay you an income, sometimes for the rest of your life.

Is the income really guaranteed?

Lifetime income options are guaranteed by the insurance company that issues the annuity. That guarantee depends on the company's financial strength and claims-paying ability. Your agent can show you the ratings of each company.

Can I lose money in an annuity?

Fixed and fixed indexed annuities don't lose value when the market falls. But most annuities have surrender charges if you take out more than allowed in the early years, and some have fees for extra features. Ask your agent to explain every charge before you decide.
